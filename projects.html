Schwinger discharge of charged primordial black holes

How fast does a charge (vacuum) black hole lose its charge? The first project of my PhD is to answer this question, with various scenarios of charged primordial black holes (PBHs) in mind. The motivation for this is that during or after their formation in the early universe, PBHs would have aquired different types of charges, such as SU(3) color charge from the surrounding quark-gluon plasma or a "dark" charge that could be associated with beyond standard model physics. We treat the discharge process as a semi-classical tunneling problem, as will soon be illustrated in an image below.
